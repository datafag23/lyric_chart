How to use:

  • Type a word in the search box and press Search (or Enter).
  • Search for multiple terms by separating them with a semicolon (e.g., word1; word2).
  • Use Include Plurals to match words like “car” and “cars”.
  • Use Albums to choose which albums are included in the search.
